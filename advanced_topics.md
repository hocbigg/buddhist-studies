### Specialization Tracks Index

*   **Advanced Buddhist Philosophy:** Dives into the rigorous logical, metaphysical, and epistemological frameworks that underpin Mahayana thought.
*   **Regional Traditions in Depth:** Focuses on the distinct scholastic, monastic, and practical developments of specific lineages rather than treating them as a monolith.
*   **Buddhism and Modernity:** Examines how Buddhism has adapted to, and been shaped by, modernism, secularism, psychology, and contemporary social movements.

### Curation Summary

*   **Advanced Buddhist Philosophy:** Included because understanding the deeper metaphysical mechanics of Mahayana is the natural next step for learners who have mastered foundational emptiness and no-self concepts. *Free-vs-paid balance:* Entirely paid. The specialized translations and philosophical commentaries required at this level (Garfield, Lusthaus, Dunne) are highly technical, and I found no adequate free video courses or open-access texts that match their scholarly depth.
*   **Regional Traditions in Depth:** Included to correct the common beginner mistake of treating "Buddhism" as a single, uniform religion by exploring how core doctrines were uniquely institutionalized across Asia. *Free-vs-paid balance:* Mixed. Includes a definitive paid monograph on Tibetan monasticism, a standard paid textbook for Theravada Abhidhamma, and an excellent free YouTube lecture series on Chinese Buddhist philosophy by a leading scholar.
*   **Buddhism and Modernity:** Included because virtually all contemporary encounters with Buddhism (both in Asia and the West) are filtered through modernist, secular, or socially engaged lenses that require historical context to understand. *Free-vs-paid balance:* Mostly paid, featuring the seminal text on Buddhist Modernism. However, it includes a highly influential, freely available academic paper critiquing the modern mindfulness movement.

## Advanced Buddhist Philosophy

An advanced exploration of the rigorous logical, metaphysical, and epistemological frameworks that underpin Mahayana thought, moving far beyond introductory concepts of emptiness and no-self.

Madhyamaka and the Logic of Emptiness: [Empty Words: Buddhist Philosophy and Cross-Cultural Dialogue by Jay L. Garfield](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780199922918) - Builds on foundational Madhyamaka by exploring how Nagarjuna's deconstructive logic intersects with Western analytic philosophy and cross-cultural dialogue.

Yogacara (Mind-Only) and Consciousness: [Buddhist Phenomenology: A Critical Investigation of Yogacara Philosophy and its Implications by Dan Lusthaus](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780415598361) - A dense, scholarly masterwork that unpacks the complex epistemological and psychological models of the Yogacara school, essential for understanding how Mahayana traditions analyze consciousness.

Buddhist Epistemology (Pramana): [Foundations of Dharmakirti's Philosophy by John D. Dunne](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780861711772) - Provides a rigorous introduction to Dharmakirti's system of valid cognition, which became the bedrock of logic and debate in later Indian and Tibetan Buddhist scholasticism.

## Regional Traditions in Depth

A focused study on the distinct scholastic, monastic, and practical developments of specific lineages, examining how core doctrines were uniquely interpreted and institutionalized across different Asian cultures.

Tibetan Scholasticism and Monastic Debate: [The Sound of Two Hands Clapping: The Education of a Tibetan Buddhist Monk by Georges Dreyfus](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780520232600) - An unparalleled insider look at the rigorous, debate-driven educational system of the Gelug tradition, revealing how Tibetan monks actually study and internalize philosophy.

East Asian Buddhism and Tiantai/Zen Philosophy: [Brook Ziporyn's Chinese Buddhism Lectures (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Brook+Ziporyn+Chinese+Buddhism) - Search for "Brook Ziporyn" on YouTube to find his extensive, university-level lecture playlists; these are the best free resources available for understanding the complex metaphysics of Tiantai, Huayan, and Chan/Zen traditions.

Theravada Abhidhamma and Meditation Theory: [A Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma edited and translated by Bhikkhu Bodhi](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781928706021) - The definitive English-language guide to the Abhidhammattha Sangaha, mapping out the intricate psychological and phenomenological taxonomy used in traditional Theravada meditation.

## Buddhism and Modernity

An examination of how Buddhism has adapted to, and been fundamentally reshaped by, encounters with modernism, secularism, scientific psychology, and contemporary social movements.

Buddhist Modernism and Historical Reform: [The Making of Buddhist Modernism by David L. McMahan](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195183276) - The seminal text explaining how 19th and 20th-century interactions with Western science, romanticism, and Protestant reform created the "modern Buddhism" familiar to most Westerners today.

The Mindfulness Movement and Secularization: [Is mindfulness Buddhist? (and why it matters) by Robert H. Sharf](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7264r9wq) - A highly influential, freely available academic paper that critically examines how modern clinical mindfulness was stripped of its traditional Buddhist ethical and soteriological context.

Engaged Buddhism and Social Action: [Socially Engaged Buddhism by Sallie B. King](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780824834050) - Explores the theological and historical roots of movements that apply Buddhist principles to contemporary social, political, and environmental justice issues.
